Lesson 1: Introduction to ICD-10-CM

Learn what ICD-10-CM is and why accurate diagnosis coding matters.

What is ICD-10-CM?

ICD-10-CM stands for International Classification of Diseases, Tenth Revision, Clinical Modification.

It is a diagnosis coding system used to report diseases, injuries, symptoms, conditions, and other reasons a patient receives healthcare.

Why is ICD-10-CM important?

Accurate ICD-10-CM coding helps healthcare providers, insurance companies, and other organizations understand why a patient received medical care.

Remember: Medical coders must choose codes based on the provider's documentation and follow official coding guidelines.

Lesson Goal

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain what ICD-10-CM means.
  • Understand what ICD-10-CM codes represent.
  • Explain why accurate diagnosis coding is important.
