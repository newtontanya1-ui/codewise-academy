Lesson 6: Orthotics and Prosthetics

Learn how HCPCS Level II codes are used to report certain orthotic and prosthetic devices.

What Are Orthotics?

Orthotic devices are used to support, protect, align, or assist a part of the body.

HCPCS Level II includes codes for many types of orthotic devices.

Examples may include:

  • Knee braces
  • Ankle-foot orthoses
  • Spinal orthoses
  • Wrist and hand orthoses
  • Other supportive devices

What Are Prosthetics?

Prosthetic devices are designed to replace or substitute for a missing body part or body function.

HCPCS Level II includes codes for certain prosthetic devices and related components.

L Codes

Many orthotic and prosthetic items are found in the HCPCS Level II L-code range.

Example format:

L1234

This demonstrates the HCPCS Level II format of one letter followed by four numbers.

Remember: Do not select an L code based only on the first letter. Always verify the complete HCPCS description for the exact device provided.

Custom vs. Prefabricated Items

Some HCPCS descriptions distinguish between different types of orthotic or prosthetic items.

The documentation should clearly describe the device provided and any details required by the applicable code.

Important: Small differences in the type of device can affect code selection, so the full code description should always be reviewed.

Medical Necessity

Documentation should support why an orthotic or prosthetic device is medically necessary for the patient.

Coverage requirements may vary depending on the device and the patient's insurance plan.

Documentation Matters

Important information may include:

  • The type of device provided
  • The body part involved
  • Laterality when applicable
  • Size or specifications
  • Whether the device is custom or prefabricated when relevant
  • The patient's medical need for the device

Example

A patient receives an orthotic device to support an injured joint.

The coder should identify the exact device, review the documentation, and verify the complete HCPCS Level II code description before assigning the code.

Quick Check

Which HCPCS Level II letter range contains many orthotic and prosthetic codes?

Answer: The L-code range.

Lesson 6 Goals

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain the basic purpose of orthotic devices.
  • Explain the basic purpose of prosthetic devices.
  • Recognize the HCPCS Level II L-code range.
  • Understand why device specifications matter.
  • Explain why medical necessity is important.
  • Understand why complete documentation is required.
