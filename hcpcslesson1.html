Lesson 1: Introduction to HCPCS Level II

Learn what HCPCS Level II codes are, what they report, and how they differ from CPT codes.

What Is HCPCS?

HCPCS stands for Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System.

HCPCS contains two main levels. CPT is commonly referred to as HCPCS Level I, while HCPCS Level II contains alphanumeric codes used for many products, supplies, and services not included in CPT.

What Does HCPCS Level II Report?

HCPCS Level II codes may be used to report items and services such as:

  • Durable medical equipment
  • Medical supplies
  • Ambulance services
  • Orthotics and prosthetics
  • Certain drugs and biologicals
  • Other healthcare products and services

HCPCS Level II Code Format

HCPCS Level II codes are generally five characters long.

They usually begin with a letter followed by four numbers.

Example format:

J1234

HCPCS Level II vs. CPT

CPT codes generally describe physician and other professional services and procedures.

HCPCS Level II codes are often used for supplies, equipment, certain medications, and other services not represented by CPT.

Easy way to remember:

CPT often describes the service or procedure.

HCPCS Level II often identifies supplies, equipment, products, or certain other services.

Why HCPCS Level II Matters

HCPCS Level II coding helps standardize reporting for many healthcare items and services.

Accurate coding depends on the documentation, the actual item or service provided, and applicable coding and payer requirements.

Quick Check

A healthcare provider supplies durable medical equipment to a patient.

Which coding system may be used to identify the equipment?

Answer: HCPCS Level II may be used to report the equipment.
