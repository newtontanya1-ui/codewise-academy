Lesson 4: Anesthesia

Learn the basic purpose of CPT anesthesia codes and important concepts used when reporting anesthesia services.

What Is Anesthesia?

Anesthesia is used to help control pain and other responses during medical or surgical procedures.

The CPT Anesthesia section contains codes used to report anesthesia services for procedures performed on different areas of the body.

Anesthesia CPT Codes

CPT anesthesia codes are generally organized according to the anatomical area and type of procedure being performed.

The coder should review the procedure performed, the anesthesia documentation, and the applicable CPT guidelines before selecting a code.

Remember: Always verify the complete CPT code description and applicable anesthesia guidelines.

Anesthesia Time

Time is an important component of reporting many anesthesia services.

The anesthesia record should document the information needed to determine the appropriate anesthesia time according to applicable coding and payer requirements.

Example:

An anesthesia record may include documented start and stop times that are used when calculating anesthesia time.

Base Units

Anesthesia reimbursement calculations may include base units associated with the anesthesia service.

Base unit values can vary depending on the anesthesia procedure and the applicable payment system.

Physical Status Modifiers

Physical status modifiers provide additional information about the patient's condition at the time anesthesia is provided.

These modifiers range from P1 through P6.

Important: The appropriate physical status modifier must be supported by the patient's documented condition and applicable coding guidance.

Qualifying Circumstances

In certain situations, additional information may be reported to describe special circumstances surrounding an anesthesia service.

Coders should review CPT guidance and payer rules to determine whether additional reporting is appropriate.

Documentation Matters

Accurate anesthesia coding depends on complete documentation.

The coder may need information about the procedure, anesthesia time, patient condition, provider involvement, and other circumstances of the service.

Quick Check

What do physical status modifiers provide information about?

Answer: They provide additional information about the patient's physical condition at the time anesthesia is provided.

Lesson 4 Goals

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain the basic purpose of anesthesia coding.
  • Understand that anesthesia codes relate to the procedure and anatomical area.
  • Recognize the importance of anesthesia time.
  • Understand the basic concept of base units.
  • Recognize physical status modifiers P1 through P6.
  • Explain why anesthesia documentation is important.
