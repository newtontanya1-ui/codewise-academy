Final Medical Billing Review

You have now worked through the major steps of the healthcare billing process.

Medical billing requires accurate patient information, insurance verification, correct claim information, careful payment review, and timely follow-up.

Remember: Accurate billing should always be based on supported documentation and applicable payer requirements.

Practice Question 1

What is the purpose of medical billing?

Check Answer

Medical billing helps prepare, submit, and follow up on healthcare claims for reimbursement.

Practice Question 2

What is one of the first steps in the healthcare revenue cycle?

Check Answer

Patient registration is one of the first steps.

Practice Question 3

Why is insurance verification important?

Check Answer

It helps confirm insurance information, coverage details, and applicable benefit requirements.

Practice Question 4

Which form is commonly associated with professional healthcare claims?

Check Answer

CMS-1500.

Practice Question 5

Which form is commonly associated with institutional healthcare claims?

Check Answer

UB-04.

Practice Question 6

What is claim adjudication?

Check Answer

Claim adjudication is the process a payer uses to review and process a healthcare claim.

Practice Question 7

What does EOB stand for?

Check Answer

Explanation of Benefits.

Practice Question 8

What does ERA stand for?

Check Answer

Electronic Remittance Advice.

Practice Question 9

What is the basic difference between a rejected claim and a denied claim?

Check Answer

A rejected claim generally contains an error that prevents it from being accepted for processing. A denied claim has generally been processed, but payment was not approved for some or all of the claim.

Practice Question 10

What does A/R stand for in medical billing?

Check Answer

Accounts Receivable.
