Lesson 7: HCPCS Modifiers

Learn how modifiers provide additional information about HCPCS Level II items and services.

What Is a Modifier?

A modifier provides additional information about an item, service, or the circumstances under which it was provided.

Modifiers are added to codes when appropriate and supported by documentation and coding rules.

Remember: Never add a modifier simply to increase payment or bypass a claim edit.

Why Are HCPCS Modifiers Important?

HCPCS modifiers can communicate important details that may not be contained in the basic code description.

They may provide information about:

  • Which side of the body was treated
  • Rental or purchase of equipment
  • Specific fingers or toes
  • Ambulance origin and destination
  • Other circumstances related to an item or service

Laterality Modifiers

Certain modifiers can identify the side of the body involved.

LT

Identifies the left side when applicable.

RT

Identifies the right side when applicable.

Important: The documentation must support the side reported.

Rental and Purchase Modifiers

Certain modifiers may provide information about whether durable medical equipment is being rented or purchased.

The appropriate modifier depends on the item, circumstances, and applicable payer requirements.

Finger and Toe Modifiers

HCPCS includes modifiers that can identify specific fingers and toes when required.

These modifiers provide more specific information about the anatomical location of a service.

CodeWise Tip: Always compare the modifier with the medical documentation before submitting the claim.

Ambulance Modifiers

Ambulance modifiers may identify the patient's origin and destination.

This information helps describe where the ambulance trip began and ended.

Documentation Matters

Modifier use must be supported by documentation.

Before adding a modifier, the coder should:

  • Review the code description
  • Review the medical documentation
  • Determine whether the modifier applies
  • Check applicable coding guidance
  • Review payer requirements when necessary

Quick Check

Which modifier identifies the right side when applicable?

Answer: RT

Lesson 7 Goals

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain the purpose of HCPCS modifiers.
  • Recognize LT and RT modifiers.
  • Understand that modifiers can describe rental or purchase circumstances.
  • Recognize that modifiers may identify specific fingers and toes.
  • Understand the purpose of ambulance modifiers.
  • Explain why modifier use must be supported by documentation.
