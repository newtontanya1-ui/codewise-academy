Lesson 8: Medicine

Explore the CPT Medicine section and learn about common services and procedures reported from this section.

The CPT Medicine Section

The CPT Medicine section contains codes for many medical services and procedures that are not classified in the other major CPT sections.

This section includes a wide variety of services performed by healthcare professionals.

Types of Medicine Services

Examples of services found in the Medicine section can include:

  • Immunization administration
  • Psychiatry services
  • Dialysis services
  • Cardiovascular services
  • Pulmonary services
  • Neurology and neuromuscular services
  • Physical medicine and rehabilitation
  • Special services and procedures

Immunizations

CPT includes codes related to immunization administration.

Depending on the circumstances, reporting the administration service and reporting the vaccine or product itself may involve different codes.

Remember: Always review the documentation and applicable CPT instructions to determine which services and products are reportable.

Cardiovascular Services

The Medicine section includes certain cardiovascular diagnostic and therapeutic services.

Documentation should identify the service performed and provide the information needed to support the selected CPT code.

Pulmonary Services

Certain pulmonary testing and treatment services are also reported from the Medicine section.

Coders should carefully review the service performed and the applicable CPT guidelines.

Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation

The Medicine section includes codes for certain physical medicine and rehabilitation services.

Documentation may need to identify the type of service, treatment performed, and other details required by the applicable code.

Time-Based Services

Some CPT services may be reported based partly on documented time.

When a code is time-based, coders should review the applicable CPT instructions and documentation requirements before selecting the code or units.

Important: Never assume the amount of time spent providing a service. Use the documented information and applicable coding rules.

Example

A patient receives a service that is reported from the CPT Medicine section.

The coder should identify the exact service performed, review the documentation, and verify the appropriate CPT code and any applicable instructions.

Quick Check

If a CPT service is time-based, should the coder guess how much time was spent?

Answer: No. The coder should use the documented time and follow the applicable CPT coding instructions.

Lesson 8 Goals

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain the basic purpose of the CPT Medicine section.
  • Recognize common types of Medicine services.
  • Understand basic immunization reporting concepts.
  • Recognize cardiovascular and pulmonary services.
  • Recognize physical medicine and rehabilitation services.
  • Understand why documented time matters for time-based services.
  • Explain why CPT instructions must be reviewed.
